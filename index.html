Curiosity, made practical.

I follow difficult questions all the way to working systems.

I graduated summa cum laude in electrical and electronics engineering with a minor in physics, and now investigate quantum algorithms for supply-chain resilience as a graduate research assistant at Clemson University.

Quantum computing brings together the mathematics, physics, computing, and engineering that have shaped my education.

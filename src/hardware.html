PROJECT COMPRESSOR

For my undergraduate senior project, my team was tasked with optimizing valve plate geometry for an air compressor. Despite my 2 members of my 3-person team being electrical, we were able to deliver a rubric with which future valve plates can be designed. I personally wrote MATLAB code to simulate the fluid dynamics of the valve plate for modelling. https://github.com/Centiganda851/Compressor-simulator.git

EXPERIMENT TUNNEL

2024's Experiment Tunnel was a project designed to measure the drag force on a moving test obstacle strapped to a car flying at 40mph. We made use of load cells and bluetooth arduinos to collect data in real time.

PROJECT MARCHING BADGE

The Marching badge is the peak of my CAD knowledge and perfect synchronization. Designed in Creo Parametric, it features a complex gear system rotating around several axes. I might consider 3D printing and assembling it in the future. (2023)

EXPERIMENT PLANCK

In 2024, conducted an experiment to determine Planck's constant using Daedalon photoelectric effect apparatus. Got a value of 5.8*10^24Js which is within 12.5% of the accepted 6.626*10^-34Js. (2023)

PROJECT RPS RPG

The RPS RPG is a protoboard-based project designed for the sole purpose of having fun. By the power of digital logic: IFs, ANDs and ORs, comes the ability to take part in a 2-player action-adventure strategic Role-Playing Game in which your probability of losing to your opponent is always much larger than your chances of success. To win, you will have to be able to predict and outwit your opponent. If you lose all 4 of your lives, you lose. (2023)
